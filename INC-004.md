# INC-1012 — Sales Portal Access Failure

## Environment

- Microsoft Entra ID
- Microsoft Graph PowerShell
- Entra security groups
- Group-based application/resource access
- PowerShell 7
- Microsoft Graph delegated authentication

### Relevant Objects

- User: Daniel Kim
- UPN: `daniel.kim@adensvaygmail.onmicrosoft.com`
- Department: Sales
- Security Group: `SG-Sales`
- Comparison User: Ethan Williams

---

## User Report

Daniel Kim reported that he could no longer access the company's Sales Portal.

The displayed error was:

> "You don't have access to this resource. Contact your administrator to request access."

Daniel stated that the resource had previously worked and that another Sales employee, Ethan Williams, could still access it.

Ticket status was changed:

`NEW → ACTIVE`

---

## Initial Investigation

Because Daniel could still authenticate to his Microsoft account, the issue appeared more likely to involve **authorization** rather than authentication.

The issue also appeared to have a limited scope:

- Daniel could authenticate successfully.
- Daniel could not access the Sales Portal.
- Ethan Williams could still access the same resource.
- The Sales Portal uses Entra group-based authorization.

This suggested investigating the user's Entra group memberships rather than immediately troubleshooting passwords, devices, or network connectivity.

---

## Investigation

I inspected the membership of the Entra security group:

`SG-Sales`

Other expected Sales users, including Ethan Williams, were members of the group.

Daniel Kim's expected membership was missing.

This provided a useful working comparison:

`Ethan → SG-Sales → Access works`

`Daniel → Missing SG-Sales membership → Access denied`

The evidence indicated that the problem was related to authorization through Entra group membership.

---

## Root Cause

Daniel Kim had lost his membership in the `SG-Sales` Entra security group.

The Sales Portal relied on this group to determine whether the user was authorized to access the resource.

Daniel's identity was still valid and authentication was working, but the authorization relationship required for the Sales resource was missing.

### Authentication vs. Authorization

This incident reinforced an important distinction:

**Authentication**

> "Who are you?"

Daniel could successfully authenticate with his Microsoft identity.

**Authorization**

> "What are you allowed to access?"

Daniel's missing `SG-Sales` membership prevented him from accessing the Sales resource.

Therefore, resetting Daniel's password would not have addressed the root cause.

---

## Resolution

After verifying the expected access relationship, Daniel was restored to the `SG-Sales` security group.

The expected relationship became:

`Daniel Kim → SG-Sales → Sales Portal authorization`

Rather than broadening permissions or modifying the application, the existing known-good group membership was restored.

Ticket status was changed:

`ACTIVE → AWAITING CLIENT`

---

## Validation

Daniel was asked to retry the Sales Portal and report any additional errors.

The user confirmed:

> "That fixed it! I signed out and back in and I can access the Sales Portal again."

Successful user validation confirmed that restoring the Entra group membership resolved the issue.

Ticket status:

`AWAITING CLIENT → RESOLVED`
